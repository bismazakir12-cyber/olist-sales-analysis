# Olist E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of a Brazilian online marketplace using Python, SQL and Power BI.

## Business Problem
Olist's management wants to understand sales trends, top categories and regions,
delivery performance, and whether late deliveries lead to poor customer reviews.

## Dataset
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle), about 100,000 orders from 2016 to 2018.
Download: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
